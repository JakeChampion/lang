Packet fixtures use independent Apple ALAC and FFmpeg decoders.
Apple source pin: c38887c5c5e64a4b31108733bd79ca9b2496d987.
https://github.com/macosforge/alac/tree/c38887c5c5e64a4b31108733bd79ca9b2496d987
The Apple decoder has an Apache-2.0 license and is a reference-only dependency.
scripts/audio-reference/alac-packets.cpp calls its public decoder interface.
It is not part of the Fern runtime.

The first-order predictor with order 31 terminates after its delta pass.
Mode 15 runs the delta pass before the declared predictor, including order zero.
These cases follow Apple's reference decoder.

The reference harness maps ALAC channels to the documented WAVE speaker order.
FFmpeg uses back-surround names for the same ALAC surround pair.
The seven-channel and eight-channel cross-checks explicitly reorder these channels before comparison.
The fixture generator changes no sample value.
FFmpeg does not implement every synthetic case.
Apple supplies independent 32-bit, two-pass, and ancillary-element PCM in those cases.
