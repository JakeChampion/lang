examples/jq/tests/jq.test is the test suite of jq 1.7, and
examples/jq/tests/modules holds the modules that the suite imports.
Reference: https://github.com/jqlang/jq/tree/jq-1.7/tests

The PRELUDE of examples/jq/filter.fern holds the jq definitions of some
builtins from src/builtin.jq of jq 1.7.
