# HTTP/1.1 hello at 4,096 connections, 2026-10-29

The hello row of #12887 at 4,096 connections. The nightly of 2026-10-28
(run 40673650834) had Fern at 68,283 requests per second and h2o at
85,494, so Fern was 20% behind.

## The cause

`serve.supervise` gave its four workers one shared listener. oha opens
its 4,096 connections in a burst. The worker that an accept wakes takes
connections from a queue that is never empty while the others sleep. A
kept-alive connection stays on the worker that took it. So one
worker holds most of the connections for the whole load.

Each worker's
open descriptors during a run here: 1,840, 779, 591 and 926.

## The change

On Linux and Android, with more than one worker, the supervisor binds
one `SO_REUSEPORT` listener for each worker before the first fork. It
keeps them open. A filter on the group sends a connection to the
listener whose index is the CPU the connection arrives on. A dead
worker's listener goes to its replacement, so its queue is not lost
(`docs/CRASH-ONLY-SERVE.md`).

## Measured on this box

A 4-core container. `scripts/net-bench.d/hello.fern` with four workers,
oha for 6 s at 4,096 connections, and h2o 2.2.5 with the lane's mruby
hello and `num-threads: 4`. Three rounds, in turn, with nothing else
running:

| round | shared listener | per worker, by hash | per worker, by CPU | h2o |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 127,167 | 122,429 | 142,877 | 141,092 |
| 2 | 126,713 | 130,766 | 157,701 | 134,211 |
| 3 | 128,925 | 136,144 | 158,359 | 129,896 |

The p99 goes from 86–115 ms with the shared listener to 67–84 ms with a
listener per worker that the CPU steers. h2o's p99 is
15–19 ms.

A listener per worker that the kernel picks by hash splits the
connections as evenly, but it gains little. The gain comes from
keeping a connection on the CPU it arrives on. So the change keeps the
filter.

## At 64 connections

The same box, two rounds of 6 s each:

| round | shared listener | per worker, by CPU |
|--:|--:|--:|
| 1 | 170,123, p99 3.4 ms | 324,979, p99 0.45 ms |
| 2 | 191,288, p99 1.5 ms | 329,266, p99 0.46 ms |

With the shared listener, two of the four workers held almost all 64
connections. With a listener per worker, each worker holds 16.
