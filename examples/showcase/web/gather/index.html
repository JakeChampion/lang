Your player has a white outline. Touch a seed or destination to move there. Nearby seeds snap to your touch. A new round starts ten seconds after the current one ends.

Participant counts reflect live connections. The arena runs in one server process and resets on restart. Multiple instances need connection affinity.

How it works

Fern validates destinations, limits movement speed, and checks seed collection. An 8 × 8 grid limits collision checks. A shared clock creates ten binary snapshots per second. Every connection uses the same snapshot bytes. Slow connections skip older snapshots. The browser predicts local movement and extrapolates nearby movement for at most 150 milliseconds.
