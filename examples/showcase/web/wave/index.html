How Fern makes this work

Your URL describes the notes, tempo, and oscillator. Fern validates that pattern, generates a 16-bit PCM waveform, applies a short envelope to avoid clicks, and writes the RIFF/WAV headers. The browser plays those server-generated bytes. An ETag lets an unchanged pattern reuse the same response.
